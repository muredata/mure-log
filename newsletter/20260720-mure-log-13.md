Intelligence is now free, so the bottleneck moves to data systems: data systems _for_ / _of_ / _by_ agents. This issue highlights some key articles: catalog and interoperability wars (DuckLake, Snowflake vs. Databricks, Grab's HMS migration), coding-agent benchmarks on real codebases, and the recurrent distributed-vs-single-node debate. The ecosystem keeps re-shuffling around the agentic era.

## Our feed

**01 / [Intelligence is Free, Now What? Data Systems for, of, and by Agents](https://bair.berkeley.edu/blog/2026/07/07/intelligence-is-free-now-what/)** — Aditya G. Parameswaran et al.
Explores what near-free intelligence means for data systems, examining systems designed for agents, systems composed of agents, and systems built by agents.

**02 / [DuckLake Architecture Deep Dive](https://motherduck.com/blog/ducklake-architecture-deep-dive/)** — Alex Monahan
Architectural details and comprehensive analysis of DuckLake.

**03 / [Benchmarking Coding Agents on Databricks' Multi-Million Line Codebase](https://www.databricks.com/blog/benchmarking-coding-agents-databricks-multi-million-line-codebase)** — Vinay Gaba et al.
Methodology and findings from an internal coding agent benchmark.

**04 / [Scaling Grab's Data Lake: Our journey to Apache Iceberg adoption](https://engineering.grab.com/our-journey-to-apache-iceberg-adoption)** — Rahul Penti
Chronicles the migration from Hive Metastore to Apache Iceberg.

**05 / [Open Interoperability: Delivering Customer Choice](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/bidirectional-interoperability-snowflake-horizon-databricks/)** — Carlos Nai and Jim Lebonitte
Compares Snowflake Horizon versus Databricks Unity Catalog on interoperability. [Additional testing referenced](https://x.com/mim_djo/status/2078488158023688701).

**06 / [How we built our knowledge base](https://www.cerebras.ai/blog/how-we-built-our-knowledge-base)** — Isaac Tai et al.
Cerebras's internal knowledge base system for connecting people and resources to relevant information.

**07 / [Running dbt on Amazon EMR Serverless with Spark Connect and S3 Tables](https://medium.com/@neuw84/running-dbt-on-amazon-emr-serverless-with-spark-connect-and-s3-tables-41d1b5a94e27)** — Angel Conde
Practical implementation combining EMR Serverless, S3, Airflow, and dbt using Spark Connect.

**08 / [Benchmarking single node vs distributed](https://pola.rs/posts/single-node-vs-distributed/)** — Chiel Peters and Ritchie Vink
Performance comparison between distributed Polars and single-node implementations.
