Hey, welcome back!

Apache Iceberg and agentic data engineering are everywhere. Unity Catalog OSS goes native on Iceberg, Snowflake's pg_lake turns Postgres into an Iceberg REST catalog, and AWS explains how Redshift handles concurrent writes to Iceberg tables. An open format is a good start, but the catalog is the real decision ([the catalog wars](https://lnkd.in/p/etWxvfNA)? [the catalog is the decision?](https://medium.com/@aniljtech/the-catalog-is-the-decision-testing-compute-agnostic-iceberg-across-three-engines-3506e8aa2a58)). In this issue: DuckDB 2.0 LTS, storage for a billion ChatGPT users, remote shuffle at LinkedIn, Boomi acquiring Elementary, a wave of agent harnesses, and more.
