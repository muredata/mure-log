Data infrastructure keeps re-forming around agents. In this issue: Snowflake's multi-engine lakehouse vision, DuckLake hitting production, a homegrown DuckDB transpiler, frontier labs starting to compete with proprietary company data, and a new context layer from MotherDuck built to make data legible to agents, not just humans.

## Our feed

**01 / [An Architect's Guide to Multi-Engine Lakehouses: What's Solved and What Isn't](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/engineering/multi-engine-lakehouse-interoperability/#snowflake-blog-author-chip-title)** — Jason Hughes and Jim Lebonitte
Multi-engine lakehouses and data interoperability explored through Snowflake's vision. Related: ["From Iceberg to Intelligence: The AI-Ready Borderless Lakehouse with Snowflake and Google Cloud"](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/snowflake-google-cloud-open-lakehouse/) by Saurin Shah et al., [Julien on the multi-engine data stack](https://juhache.substack.com/p/multi-engine-data-stack-v1), [Wes on composable data systems](https://wesmckinney.com/blog/looking-back-15-years/), and [Aitor on the headless lakehouse](https://medium.com/microsoft/headless-lakehouse-63b0a5d27068).

**02 / [The life and times of data teams in the AI era](https://hex.tech/blog/data-teams-in-ai-era/)** — Katie Bauer
AI reshapes data team workflows and approaches to data utilization.

**03 / [DuckLake in Production - Catalog and Storage](https://thefulldatastack.substack.com/p/ducklake-in-production-catalog-storage)** — Hoyt Emerson
Explores production-grade DuckLake requirements. Related: ["Self-hosting DuckDB: the road to production"](https://motherduck.com/blog/self-hosting-duckdb-road-to-production/) by Mehdi Ouazza.

**04 / [The Big AI Labs Are Suddenly Competing with Your Own Data](https://gradientflow.substack.com/p/the-big-ai-labs-are-suddenly-competing)** — Ben Lorica
Examines how company data enables specialized intelligence development.

**05 / [Indexing the Data Lake for Online Point Queries](https://engineering.atspotify.com/2026/7/indexing-the-data-lake-for-online-point-queries)** — Spotify R&D
Techniques for single I/O point lookups in Apache Parquet-based data lakes.

**06 / [How we built a DuckDB transpiler](https://www.cocoalemana.com/blog/building-a-duckdb-transpiler/)** — Coco Alemana
Creating a DuckDB dialect subset that transpiles to other SQL dialects.
