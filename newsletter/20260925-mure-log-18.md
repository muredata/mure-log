Jev broke the internet. Will it break data next? Almost certainly. After Apache Spark, ClickHouse and DuckDB opened up new use cases, and Jev is set to do the same. In this issue: what Jev means for data engineering, building a harness with Jev and LangChain, the agentic data stack, single-node Iceberg benchmarks, the DAVE stack, spark-vector, multi-engine architectures, and more.

## Our feed

**01 / [What Jev will do to data engineering](https://www.astronomer.io/blog/what-jev-will-do-to-data-engineering/)** - Julian LaNeve and Kaxil Naik
Fantastic read. See also: [More on Jev](https://www.bensbites.com/p/what-can-you-build-with-jev) by Ben Tossell.

**02 / [Building a harness with Jev](https://x.com/sydneyrunkle/status/2100754364545761643)** - Sydney Runkle
How to use Jev with LangChain.

**03 / [Beyond the Semantic Layer: Engineering the Agentic Data Stack](https://medium.com/@Joannahe/beyond-the-semantic-layer-engineering-the-agentic-data-stack-98fbaee9ad10)** - Joanna He
Proposes a five-layer agentic data stack: L1 data estate and connectors, L2 sandbox and safety, L3 semantic layer and ontology, L4 data agent and reasoning harness, L5 data applications and marketplace.

**04 / [Rapidly scaling online storage to serve over 1 billion ChatGPT users](https://openai.com/index/scaling-storage-one-billion-users-part-one/)** - Jon Lee et al.
OpenAI's online storage by the numbers: 70M requests per second, 1B weekly users, 500 PB of data, 40 regions. Good read.

**05 / [The DAVE stack: the age of domain-optimized analytical databases on Datafusion, Arrow, and Vortex, Embedded](https://sequenceandsilicon.substack.com/p/the-dave-stack-the-age-of-domain)** - Tim Poterba
How to build a domain-optimized OLAP database with the DAVE stack.

**06 / [Iceberg on a single node is coming together](https://datamonkeysite.com/2026/09/21/iceberg-on-a-single-node-is-coming-together/)** - Mim
DuckDB for the win.

**07 / [The Rise of Multi-Query Engines | How AI opens up more options for better querying](https://dataopsleadership.substack.com/p/the-rise-of-multi-query-engines-how)** - Hugo Lu
Agents + Iceberg + a catalog + an engine. See also the [related discussion](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lakehouse_curious-to-hear-the-community-thoughts-on-share-7508400163317727232-AmNB/) started by Kyle Weller.
