Hey, welcome back!

Apache Iceberg is the data king. Who will be the context queen?

The single-node vs. distributed debate is back. PostHog is moving its warehouse from DuckDB to Trino after reaching 2PB, while MotherDuck's Jordan Tigani says most workloads never needed a cluster. Scale decides the architecture, and storage matters too: local NVMe beats network-attached disks. In this issue: DuckDB inside DataGrip, the case for a Databricks lakehouse, moving from BigQuery to Iceberg, Spark 4.3, dbt v2, ClickHouse acquiring Xata, EDB acquiring Altinity, Hex's $100M raise, and more.
