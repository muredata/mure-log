DuckLabs is joining AWS, with DuckDB staying open source. S3 (or another object store) plus Iceberg plus an engine is all you need, or maybe not, as a Hyparquet-and-Parquet-on-R2 experiment below shows. This issue covers fast dashboards off a single Parquet file on R2, dbt running over Spark Connect with Iceberg v3 and the new VARIANT type, GPU-accelerated Spark benchmarks against CPU, and why AI-generated charts look gorgeous right up until the numbers are wrong. Also: MotherDuck acquires Tower.dev, DuckDB v2.0 gets a preview, and Apache DataFusion hits 55.0.
